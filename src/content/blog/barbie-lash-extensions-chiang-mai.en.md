## What are Barbie lash extensions?

"Barbie" is the name of a look, not a fixed technique, so every studio does it a little differently. Here is what it means at ours:

- **A wispy base.** Full volume fans with longer spikes placed in a rhythm, so the lash line has texture instead of a flat strip.
- **Fluffier than our regular wispy.** Extra density at the base makes the eyes look darker, rounder and more doll-like.
- **A long, lifted outer corner.** The longest lengths sit at the outer third and lift the eye in a cat-eye direction.
- **Optional pink accents.** A few colored lashes for the full Barbie effect, or keep it all black.

Lengths usually run 10 to 15 mm. Ying picks them based on how long and strong your natural lashes are, because a set heavier than your natural lashes can carry sheds early and damages them. We don't do that.

## How long do Barbie lashes last?

A well-applied set looks its best for 2 to 3 weeks, then a refill brings back the fullness. Dense, fluffy sets need a daily wash with an oil-free foam cleanser, because tightly packed fans trap makeup and skin oil. The full routine is in the [lash extension aftercare guide](/blog/lash-extension-aftercare-chiang-mai).

If lashes shed abnormally in the first 3 days because of the glue or the application, message us photos on Instagram and we retouch them free.
