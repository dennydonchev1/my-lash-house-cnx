## Common questions about lash extensions in Chiang Mai

### How much do lash extensions cost in Chiang Mai?

A full set in Chiang Mai can cost anything from a few hundred baht to well over a thousand, depending on the style, the fans and the studio. My Lash House publishes ฿590 to ฿1,590 across the full menu; the [lash extension price guide](/blog/lash-extension-prices-chiang-mai) explains why prices vary so much between studios. For the other three studios, ask for a quote when you book. Whatever the price, ask whether the fans are handmade or pre-made before booking; it is one of the strongest predictors of retention and natural-lash health.

### How long do lash extensions last?

A well-applied set lasts 2 to 4 weeks before a refill, with the natural shedding cycle of your real lashes setting the limit. You shed a few natural lashes every day, and an extension goes with each one. Heavier styles (mega volume) shed faster than lighter styles (classic) because of the extra weight. Side-sleepers and people often in the water shed faster too. With My Lash House's 3-day retouch guarantee, unusual early shedding caused by the glue or the application is covered. After that, book a refill every 2 to 4 weeks to keep the look full.

### Are lash extensions safe?

Lash extensions are safe when applied by a careful artist using fans matched to the strength of your natural lashes. Risks come from rushed isolation (natural lashes glued together fall out together and damage real lashes), fans that are too heavy for the natural lash, allergic reactions to the cyanoacrylate adhesive (rare but possible), and poor aftercare. Tell your artist about any sensitivities, ask whether they isolate one lash at a time, and ask about a patch test if you're worried. The biggest safety factor is choosing an artist who isolates carefully, not choosing the cheapest salon.

### What's the difference between classic, hybrid, volume, and mega-volume lash extensions?

- **Classic:** one extension per natural lash. Subtle, mascara-like effect. Lightest. Best for first-timers and natural looks.
- **Hybrid:** a mix of classic single extensions and light handmade fans. More texture than classic, less drama than volume. The everyday middle ground.
- **Volume:** handmade fans (3 to 6 thin extensions per fan) attached to one natural lash. Full, fluffy, photogenic. Heavier than classic.
- **Mega volume:** larger handmade fans (10 to 16 extensions per fan). The most dramatic and the heaviest. Needs strong natural lashes and careful application. Not for everyone.

If you're new to extensions, start with classic or hybrid. Save mega volume for strong natural lashes and clients who specifically want the look.

### Are lash extensions worth it?

Lash extensions don't suit everyone. Decide whether they fit your routine before choosing a salon.

**Reasons to get lash extensions:**

- **Time savings.** No daily mascara, no eyelash curler, fewer steps in the morning.
- **Look-good factor.** Wider, fuller, more open-looking eyes with no makeup.
- **Photo-ready.** Especially helpful for travelers, brides, or anyone photographed often.
- **Confidence.** Many clients feel a real confidence lift in the first few days.
- **No mascara smudging.** Especially useful in Thailand's heat and humidity.

**Reasons to think twice:**

- **Cost over time.** Extensions need a refill every 2 to 4 weeks, and the yearly cost adds up. Set a budget you're comfortable with before you start.
- **Time.** A full set takes about 1.5 to 2.5 hours; refills take less.
- **Lifestyle adjustments.** Avoid oil-based cleansers, and follow your studio's water rule: 24 to 48 hours with standard adhesive, as little as 2 hours with fast-cure glue (what My Lash House uses). Side-sleepers may shed faster.
- **Risk if done poorly.** Bad application can damage natural lashes, especially with rushed isolation or fans that are too heavy. Choosing a careful artist matters more than choosing a cheap one.
- **Allergic reactions.** A small percentage of clients react to lash adhesive. Tell your artist about any sensitivities before your first set, and ask about a patch test if you're worried.

The honest take: lash extensions are worth it if you can budget for the upkeep (money and lifestyle) and choose an artist who isolates carefully and matches the fans to your natural lashes. They aren't worth it if you'd resent the maintenance or the cost. There's no shame in deciding lashes aren't for you. A lash lift, mascara, or no makeup at all are all fine options.

## What should I ask before booking lash extensions?

Before committing to any lash artist in Chiang Mai, ask these three questions:

**Are the fans handmade or pre-made?** Handmade fans are sized to each natural lash. Pre-made fans can be heavier and shed faster, especially on weak or thin natural lashes. If a salon uses pre-made fans, the price should be lower. That's the trade-off.

**Is isolation done one lash at a time?** Proper application isolates each natural lash before applying an extension. If natural lashes get glued together because the artist rushed, they fall out together and can damage your real lashes.

**What's the retouch or damage policy?** Some studios offer a free retouch within a certain window if a set sheds unusually fast. If a studio has no policy at all, that's worth weighing.

If a studio can't or won't answer these clearly, that itself is information.
