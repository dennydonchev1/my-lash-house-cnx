## What are wispy lash extensions?

Three things separate a wispy set from ordinary volume:

1. **Mixed lengths.** Longer spikes sit between shorter fans, which gives the lash line texture, like natural lashes at a bigger scale.
2. **A dense base.** Volume fans darken the root so it reads like eyeliner. Most clients stop lining their eyes.
3. **A lifted outer corner.** The longest spikes sit toward the outer third, so the eye looks longer and sharper.

The result photographs beautifully, and with the right lengths it still works every day.

## How do I pick a wispy set for my eye shape?

A wispy set lives or dies on mapping, meaning which length goes where. Density matters less than placement.

**Visible double eyelid.** Every style works. Wispy Volume shows off most because the spikes stay in view.

**Monolid or hooded eyes.** Short spikes disappear under the lid. The fix is a curl that lifts higher and lengths that clear the lid, which Ying plans at consultation. Very doable; it takes more careful design.

**Round eyes.** Longest lengths toward the outer corner, cat-eye style, to elongate.

**Small eyes.** Hybrid or a mid-length Wispy Volume. Lashes that are too long or heavy cover the eye instead of opening it.

## How long do wispy lash extensions last?

A wispy set looks fullest in the first two weeks. After that your natural lashes shed on their normal cycle, so a refill every 2 to 3 weeks keeps the spikes looking neat. Heavier styles like Mega Volume shed slightly faster because each natural lash carries more weight, which makes good aftercare matter more. Week-by-week detail is in [how long lash extensions last](/blog/how-long-do-lash-extensions-last-chiang-mai).

## Can I wear wispy lashes to work?

Yes, with the right lengths. Hybrid or a mid-length Wispy Volume reads as great lashes rather than full glam. Mega Volume and long Wet Look sets suit special days better. For a very conservative workplace, start with the [natural lash extensions guide](/blog/natural-lash-extensions-chiang-mai).

## How do I care for wispy lashes?

The studio uses a fast-cure adhesive, so you can shower 2 hours after your appointment. After that:

- Cleanse daily with an oil-free lash foam. Dense sets trap more makeup than light ones.
- Brush gently each morning so the spikes fall back into place.
- Sleep on your back or use a silk pillowcase; sleeping face-down flattens spikes fast.
- Don't rub your eyes or pull at loose lashes.
