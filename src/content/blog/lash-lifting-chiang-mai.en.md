## What is lash lifting, exactly?

Lash lifting is the modern version of a lash perm. A silicone shield is placed on your eyelid, your natural lashes are combed up over the shield, and a gentle keratin-based lifting lotion is applied to the base for 8 to 12 minutes. A setting solution locks the new curl in place, and the whole thing takes about 45 minutes.

You leave with your own natural lashes curled straight up, looking noticeably longer and more open, without any synthetic material added. Add-on tint deepens the color for a mascara-like effect.

## What's the difference between lash lifting and lash extensions?

The two services solve different problems.

| | Lash Lifting | Lash Extensions |
|---|---|---|
| What's added | Nothing (curls your own lashes) | Handmade fans glued to each natural lash |
| Duration | 6 to 8 weeks | 3 to 4 weeks (with refills every 2 to 3 weeks) |
| Maintenance | None between visits | Daily cleaning + refills |
| Look | "My lashes but curled" | "Fuller, darker, more defined" |
| Time in chair | ~45 minutes | 90 to 180 minutes for a full set |
| Cost cycle | One appointment, no refills | Full set + regular refills |
| Best for | Naturally long but straight lashes | Adding volume, length, or drama |
| Cure window | Same 24-hour rule | Same 24-hour rule |

Neither is "better." Lifts win for low-maintenance, no-commitment beauty. Extensions win for adding fullness the natural lashes don't have.

## How does the lash lifting process work at My Lash House?

Every appointment follows the same seven-step sequence. Total time in the chair: about 45 minutes.

1. **Consultation.** Ying checks your natural lash length, condition, and growth angle. Very short or damaged lashes may not be a fit — she'll say so upfront.
2. **Cleansing.** Full eye-area cleanse with an oil-free cleanser. Any lingering makeup or skincare would block the lifting solution.
3. **Silicone shield placement.** A curved silicone pad (small, medium, or large depending on your lash length) is placed on the eyelid.
4. **Lash combing.** Your natural lashes are combed up and stuck to the shield with a temporary adhesive gel. This is the shape they'll hold.
5. **Lifting solution.** Applied to the base of the lashes only (never the tips). Sits for 8 to 12 minutes.
6. **Setting solution.** Locks in the new curl. 8 to 10 minutes.
7. **Optional tint + conditioning.** Add-on tint darkens the color; a nourishing conditioner strengthens the freshly lifted lashes.

You lie back with your eyes closed for the whole thing. Many clients fall asleep. That's fine.

## How long does a lash lift last?

A well-done lash lift lasts **6 to 8 weeks**.

The curl doesn't "drop" gradually — it fades as your natural lashes shed. You lose 2 to 5 natural lashes per day (with or without a lift), and each new lash that grows in comes in straight because it wasn't in the solution.

By week 4 the curl is still visible but softer. By week 6 to 8, about half your lash line is new growth without the lift, and it's time to book again.

There's no refill for a lift. The next appointment is a fresh treatment. This is a big part of why lifts are the low-maintenance option compared to extensions.

## What are the pros and cons of lash lifting?

The honest breakdown.

**Pros:**

- No commitment. One appointment, 6 to 8 weeks of results, done.
- Zero daily maintenance. No brushing, no lash shampoo, no aftercare rules past the first 24 hours.
- Natural look. Reads as "my lashes on a good day" rather than styled.
- Suits sensitive eyes better than extensions for many clients (no adhesive touching the lash line for weeks).
- Safe during pregnancy for most women (check with your OB, but no cyanoacrylate exposure).
- Great pre-vacation prep — one appointment, no maintenance while you travel.

**Cons:**

- Requires healthy, reasonably long natural lashes. Very short or brittle lashes may not lift dramatically.
- The result varies by lash length. If you want visible extra length, extensions are the better choice.
- Can't fix asymmetry the way custom-mapped extensions can. The lift follows the shape your lashes already grow in.
- Some clients find waterproof mascara doesn't play well with lifted lashes (residue is harder to clean off).
- If a shop uses harsh solutions or over-processes, lashes can dry out or break. Choosing a careful artist matters as much as it does for extensions.

## Who is lash lifting right for?

Some client patterns come up on the chair over and over.

**People with naturally long but straight lashes.** The best-case-scenario client. The lift takes what's already there and just opens it up.

**Pregnant or breastfeeding women.** Many avoid extension adhesive during pregnancy. A lift has no cyanoacrylate exposure at the lash line.

**Low-maintenance beauty preference.** No morning brushing, no lash shampoo, no refill schedule.

**Anyone booked to travel.** Get lifted, then travel worry-free for 6 to 8 weeks.

**Clients whose eyes have reacted to extensions.** Some sensitivities are triggered by the adhesive, not the fans. A lift avoids that entirely.

**First-timers testing the waters.** Lower commitment, lower cost per appointment, less scary than committing to a 3-week extension cycle.

## How do I care for lifted lashes?

Simpler than extension aftercare, but the same basic curing rules apply.

**First 24 hours.** No water on the lashes, no steam, no makeup, no sleeping face-down. The lifting solution needs a full day to set.

**First week.** Avoid oil-based skincare directly on the lash line. Oil doesn't dissolve a lift the way it dissolves extension adhesive, but it can over-condition the lashes and soften the curl.

**Daily forever.** Gentle cleansing with a water-based cleanser. That's really it. No lash shampoo, no daily brushing needed. If you want, a swipe of clear brow gel or lash serum on the tips (never the base) keeps things looking polished.
