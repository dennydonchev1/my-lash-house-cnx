# Lower Lash Extensions in Chiang Mai: Who They Suit and What to Expect (2026)

*By Ying, certified lash artist · My Lash House · Published November 14, 2026*

Most people only think about the top row. Then they see a photo where the eyes look wide, awake and somehow finished, and the secret is sitting on the bottom lash line.

Lower lash extensions are small, quick to add and easy to get wrong. This guide covers what they are, who they flatter, who should skip them, how long they last and what they cost.

## The short answer

Lower lash extensions are short, fine extensions placed one by one on your bottom lashes. They open the eye, balance a full upper set and replace lower mascara. They suit round and wide-set eyes, sparse lower lashes and anyone who wants a doll-eyed finish. They shed faster than upper lashes because the lower lash line meets tears, skincare and your pillow. ## What are lower lash extensions?

They are the same idea as upper extensions, scaled down. Your lower lashes are shorter, finer and fewer than the top row, so the extensions are too. Artists generally use short lengths, around 5 to 7 mm, in a light classic placement: one extension on one natural lash.

The goal is definition, not drama. A good lower set looks like you were born with a slightly better lash line. Nobody should be able to point at it.

## Who do lower lashes suit?

- **Round or large eyes.** Lower lashes frame the whole eye and push the look toward doll-eyed.
- **Sparse or very light lower lashes.** If you reach for lower mascara every morning, this replaces it, with no smudging by lunch.
- **A full upper set.** Volume on top with nothing below can look top-heavy. A few lower lashes balance it.
- **Photos and events.** Weddings, graduations, trips. The camera picks up the lower lash line more than you would expect.

## Who should skip them?

Honest answer: not everyone needs them.

- **Watery or sensitive eyes.** Tears sit on the lower lash line and shorten the life of the bond.
- **A heavy under-eye routine.** Rich eye cream, oil cleansers and concealer all live exactly where the extensions sit.
- **Face-down sleepers.** The pillow wins.
- **First-timers who feel nervous.** Get comfortable with an upper set first. Lower lashes can join at your next refill.

## How long do lower lash extensions last?

Shorter than the top row. Lower lashes meet tears, skincare and friction all day, so expect them to thin out sooner than your upper set. Most people top them up at their regular refill, every 2 to 3 weeks. The full timeline for upper sets is in [how long do lash extensions last](/blog/how-long-do-lash-extensions-last-chiang-mai).

## How to care for lower lash extensions

1. Keep oil away from the lower lash line. That includes eye cream, cleansing balm and oil-based concealer.
2. Pat dry, never rub. Press a towel gently under the eye.
3. Clean them. A soft lash cleanser keeps the line fresh and the bond strong.
4. Skip lower mascara. You no longer need it, and removing it pulls the extensions off.
